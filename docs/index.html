African Harmonised Early-Grade Assessments Data

The AHEAD Study Catalogue provides comprehensive documentation for every study included in the African Harmonised Early-Grade Assessment (AHEAD) dataset. It brings together harmonised metadata on each study enabling researchers to understand the context, scope, and comparability of each constituent survey. The catalogue also includes links to original data sources, technical documentation, and guidance on the use of the harmonised dataset, supporting transparent and reproducible cross-country research on foundational literacy and numeracy in Africa.
