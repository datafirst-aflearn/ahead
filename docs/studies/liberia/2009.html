2009 EGRA Plus

From 2008 to 2010, EGRA Plus, a project funded by the United States Agency for International Development (USAID) and the World Bank and implemented by RTI International in collaboration with the Liberian Ministry of Education, evaluated the effectiveness of interventions designed to improve early grade reading instruction in public and community primary schools. Data were collected using the Early Grade Reading Assessment (EGRA) in English during baseline (2008), midline (2009), and endline (2010) surveys. The study was implemented as a cluster randomized controlled trial (RCT) with a school-level panel design, in which the same schools were followed over time while new cross-sectional samples of Grade 2 and Grade 3 learners were assessed at each survey round. Schools were randomly assigned to one of three intervention arms: a Full Treatment group that received phonics-based teacher training, instructional support, and reading materials; a Light Treatment group that received an information-based intervention focused on school accountability and student assessment; and a Control group that continued with the standard national curriculum. The evaluation was designed to estimate the causal impact of these interventions on early grade reading outcomes rather than to produce nationally representative estimates.

Sampling Description

The EGRA Plus evaluation employed a two-stage cluster sampling design within a cluster randomized controlled trial and was designed to estimate the causal impact of the intervention rather than produce nationally representative estimates. The sampling frame comprised public and community primary schools across Liberia. In the first stage, 176 schools (from a target of 180) were selected and grouped into geographic clusters within districts for treatment randomization. Clusters were randomly assigned to one of three study arms: Full Treatment (59 schools), Light Treatment (60 schools), or Control (57 schools). The same schools were followed throughout the 2008 baseline, 2009 midline, and 2010 endline surveys, while a new sample of pupils was selected independently at each survey round. In the second stage, between 10 and 20 Grade 2 and Grade 3 pupils were selected per school using systematic random sampling from pupils present on the day of assessment, with a target of assessing at least 10 pupils from each grade where possible. The harmonized dataset contains 2,988 pupil records from the 2008 baseline, 2,882 from the 2009 midline, and 2,688 from the 2010 endline. As this was an impact evaluation, survey estimates are representative of the study sample rather than the national pupil population, and no sampling weights are described in the evaluation documentation.
