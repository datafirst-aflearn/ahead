2015 Accès, Lecture, Redevabilité et Rétention! Activity 1 (A!1)

From 2015 to 2020, ACCELERE! (I-READ), a USAID-funded programme, sought to improve equitable access to education and strengthen foundational reading outcomes in public, religious, non-formal, and vocational schools. Learning outcomes were assessed using the Early Grade Reading Assessment (EGRA), administered in French and selected national languages. Data collection was conducted in selected sub-provinces across four of the Democratic Republic of the Congo's eleven provinces (Equateur, Kasaï Occidental, Kasaï Oriental, and Katanga), with an additional non-representative Accelerated Learning Programme (ALP) sample in Nord-Kivu and Sud-Kivu. As the study covered only selected provinces and sub-provinces, the resulting dataset is not nationally representative.

Sampling Description

The ACCELERE! Activity 1 evaluation employed a three-stage cluster sampling design. The sampling frame comprised schools participating in the ACCELERE! Activity 1 evaluation in selected provinces and programme entry cohorts, rather than all primary schools in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. In the first stage, 235 schools were selected with equal probability within strata defined by province and programme entry year, with separate strata for the Accelerated Learning Programme (ALP) in Goma and Bukavu. In the second stage, 491 classrooms were selected, typically one Grade 3 and one Grade 5 classroom per regular school and one classroom per ALP school. In the third stage, 4,796 pupils were selected with equal probability from sampled classrooms, stratified by gender (approximately five boys and five girls per classroom); the harmonized dataset contains 4,794 pupil records. Consequently, survey estimates are representative only of the eligible pupil population within the sampled ACCELERE! schools and strata and should not be interpreted as representative of all primary school pupils in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Users should verify the appropriate survey weights and survey design variables against the original ACCELERE! Activity 1 documentation before producing published estimates.
